# Owner invariants (example)

These are the decisions the project owner has fixed. Every task packet includes
this file verbatim, so a worker cannot "forget" them and the orchestrator cannot
quietly drop them. Keep it short; it is sent with every high-tier prompt.

- INV-01: Target platform and form factor (e.g. 2D, desktop browser, one-handed play).
- INV-02: Who the user is and what they are doing.
- INV-03: The one mechanic or feature the product is built around, and what it must not turn into.
- INV-04: What the owner has already tried and rejected, in their own words.
- INV-05: Anything the owner did not fix is open for agents to research, prototype and decide on evidence.
- INV-06: AI review and simulation are not human testing. No claim of "fun", "intuitive" or
  "users want this" without human evidence.
- INV-07: Every stage leaves a build the owner can start with one command. The owner's own
  play-through outranks any AI review.
